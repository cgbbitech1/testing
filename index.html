Printing: The groove features are tens of microns deep, so you need a resin printer with fine resolution (MSLA/SLA, ≤ 35 µm XY, 25 µm layers or finer) for it to play. An FDM printer with a 0.4 mm nozzle can't reproduce the waveform. Print flat with the groove side up and no supports on the top face, then wash and cure it fully.
Playing: It spins clockwise and plays from the outside in, just like vinyl, and fits a standard 7.24 mm spindle. Use a cheap or spare stylus with a slightly higher tracking force (3–5 g), because resin wears needles. Expect lo-fi, AM-radio-like sound with surface noise. The sample rate sets the frequency ceiling (about half the rate).
